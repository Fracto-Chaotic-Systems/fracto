# Campaign framework

## Short post

```text
Hook or question.

One observation in plain language.

Optional image/animation and a question for the reader.
```

## Explainer thread

1. A question or surprising claim.
2. A visual that makes the question concrete.
3. A plain-language definition of one term.
4. The current observation or result.
5. What remains uncertain and how it will be tested.
6. A question inviting the audience to continue the investigation.

## Visual post checklist

- Does the image have useful, specific alt text?
- Is the focal phenomenon visible without reading a paragraph first?
- Is the color or motion rule explained when it matters?
- Is the image linked to a question or a discovery rather than posted alone?

## Reusable series

- **Inside the Set:** one interior phenomenon at a time.
- **Orbital Notes:** cardinality, paths, and the relationship between points.
- **Fracto Field Report:** a new artifact or unexpected observation.
- **One Point, Explained:** one focal point, one calculation, one visual result.
- **From Equation to Experience:** connect a mathematical operation to color,
  motion, sound, or an image.

## Campaign review

After each campaign, record:

- Posts and dates
- Visual assets used
- Profile visits and new followers
- Likes, replies, reposts, and quote-posts
- Questions that deserve a follow-up
- Claims that need stronger evidence or clearer wording
