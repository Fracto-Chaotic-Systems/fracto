# Fracto social communication

This folder is the working reference for public communication about the Fracto
project. It keeps the account's established voice, published material, public
responses, and reusable campaign structures together so that future posts can
build on the existing record rather than starting from memory.

## Contents

- [CONTENT_GUIDELINES.md](CONTENT_GUIDELINES.md) — voice, themes, editorial
  guardrails, and audience goals.
- [Bluesky/POST_ARCHIVE.md](Bluesky/POST_ARCHIVE.md) — the current archive of public posts and
  their associated media descriptions and engagement snapshot.
- [Bluesky/RESPONSE_LOG.md](Bluesky/RESPONSE_LOG.md) — a running record of public replies,
  quote-posts, questions, and follow-up opportunities.
- [CAMPAIGN_FRAMEWORK.md](CAMPAIGN_FRAMEWORK.md) — reusable structures for
  short posts, threads, visual posts, and multi-week campaigns.

## Source and maintenance

The initial archive was prepared from the public Bluesky author feed for
`fracto-studio.bsky.social` on 2026-09-20. Dates in the archive are the post
timestamps returned by Bluesky, in UTC. Engagement counts are a snapshot and
should not be treated as permanent historical values.

When adding material:

1. Preserve the original post text exactly in the archive.
2. Record media alt text when available; it is part of the communication record.
3. Keep observed facts separate from interpretation and campaign suggestions.
4. Record the date and source of every response or metric update.
5. Never store private messages, personal information, or identifying details
   about members of the public unless there is a clear editorial reason and
   explicit permission.
