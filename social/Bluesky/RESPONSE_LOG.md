# Public response log

This file records public replies, quote-posts, questions, and recurring themes
that can inform future communication. It is intentionally separate from the
post archive: a response is evidence about audience understanding, not merely a
metric attached to the original post.

## Current state

The initial feed snapshot recorded reply counts and the public reply threads were
checked on 2026-09-20. The replies currently attached to the four-part thread
are the author's own continuation posts, not audience responses. No external
audience replies or quote-posts were found in the retrieved feed, so no response
themes should be inferred from the counts alone. Populate the table below as
future responses are reviewed.

## Response themes to watch

- Questions that reveal an unfamiliar term, especially “orbital,” “cardinality,”
  or “inside the Set.”
- Requests for a zoom, animation, explanation, or source image.
- Confusion about whether colors are mathematical properties or a Fracto mapping.
- Comparisons to music, natural forms, astronomy, or other visual systems.
- Corrections that should be addressed publicly and added to the research notes.

## Privacy and safety

Record only publicly visible material needed for communication planning. Do not
copy private messages, personal contact information, or sensitive details into
this repository. Quote a person by handle only when attribution is useful and
appropriate; otherwise summarize the idea without identifying the individual.
